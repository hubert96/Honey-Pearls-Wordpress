Information we collect

Orders and enquiries

When you contact us, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number and message. When you place an order, we also collect the products ordered, billing details, collection arrangements, relevant order notes and payment or refund references. Website orders are for collection at our premises. A billing address may be requested for payment and order administration.

Fields marked as required are needed for the relevant form or transaction. If you leave them blank, we may be unable to handle your request or provide the service. Please include only information relevant to your enquiry or order.

Reviews and public content

If you submit a product review, we collect the name, email address, rating and review you provide. Your displayed name, rating and review may be published. Your email address stays private. The website may request a profile image from Gravatar.

We display selected public reviews from Google and Tripadvisor and content from our business social media accounts. This may include a reviewer's public name, profile image, rating, review text, date, any photos attached to the public review and a link to the original source. We obtain this material from the relevant platform or its integration service.

Website and device information

We and our service providers process technical information to deliver and protect the site. This can include IP addresses, browser and device information, request times, pages requested, errors and security events. The section on cookies and browser storage explains how we use them.

How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as needed for its purpose. The following periods are our normal retention schedule.

Minimum sales, payment, refund and accounting records
Six years from the end of the company financial year to which they relate, or longer where required by law.
Extra order instructions and other operational details
Up to 90 days after collection when the details are unnecessary for the financial record.
Unpaid, failed or cancelled orders with no payment, refund or open issue
Up to 30 days after the order is closed or cancelled.
Routine enquiries and correspondence
Up to 12 months after closure or the last substantive contact. Records needed for an order, complaint or claim follow the relevant period.
Published reviews and public social content
For as long as the content is relevant and displayed, with an annual review. We remove unneeded working copies within 30 days of deciding to withdraw the content.
Routine access and error logs under our control
Our retention target is up to 30 days.
Security logs under our control
Our retention target is up to 90 days. Relevant incident evidence may be kept longer where needed for investigation or a legal claim.
Records of privacy requests and complaints
Up to three years after closure, limited to what we need to show how we handled the matter.

We may keep specific relevant information longer where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Information removed from active systems may remain in restricted recovery backups until those copies are overwritten. Our target for this backup cycle is 30 days. We reapply relevant deletions if we restore a backup. Providers acting as separate controllers may have different retention periods.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and browser storage support the basket, checkout, security and preferences you choose. We have switched off WooCommerce's order attribution tracking. We link to Google Maps directly, so the contact page does not load an embedded map.

WooCommerce basket cookies
woocommerce_cart_hash and woocommerce_items_in_cart keep track of basket changes during your session. wp_woocommerce_session_* lets WooCommerce find your basket and lasts about 48 hours.
WooCommerce browser storage
storeApiCartData, storeApiCartHash and storeApiNonce keep the basket consistent and support checkout requests. Basket data can include address information entered at checkout. These entries are stored locally until they are cleared or replaced.
Square checkout security
Square's payment interface can use Cloudflare's __cf_bm cookie on its payment domain to distinguish people from automated traffic. In our checkout test it lasted about 30 minutes. Digital wallets may use storage managed by the wallet provider.
WordPress sessions and preferences
When you sign in, WordPress uses authentication and preference cookies for the session or until they expire. wpEmojiSettingsSupports remembers the browser's emoji display support for the session. A product review form can remember your details in this browser when you choose that option.

You can inspect, block or clear cookies and stored data in your browser. Blocking storage required by the basket, checkout or payment interface may prevent those functions from working. Where an optional feature requires consent, we will ask before enabling it.
